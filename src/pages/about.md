## About Ian

I’m Ian Mattas, a high school developer and aspiring red teamer from Ohio. I’ve spent five years learning reverse engineering and how software behaves at a low level. I own and captain [idktheflag](https://idktheflag.sh).

My interests include reverse engineering, binary exploitation, malware analysis, binary obfuscation, symbolic execution, and cryptography. I work with disassemblers and debuggers to understand how software actually runs, and build tools to make repeated analysis easier to reproduce.

I’m open to interesting security, systems, and research collaborations. Find my projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/imattas) or get in touch at [ian@mattas.net](mailto:ian@mattas.net).
