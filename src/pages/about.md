## About Ian

I’m Ian Mattas, a high school developer and aspiring red teamer from Ohio. I’ve spent five years learning reverse engineering and how software behaves at a low level. I captain [idktheflag](https://idktheflag.sh), a team I compete with in cybersecurity competitions.

My interests include reverse engineering, binary exploitation, malware analysis, binary obfuscation, symbolic execution, and cryptography. I use disassemblers and debuggers to understand how software runs, and build tools to make repeated analysis easier to reproduce.

## Programming Languages

I work with Rust, C, C++, Assembly, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, and C#.
